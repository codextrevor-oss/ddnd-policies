## Voice opt-in and privacy

Being in a voice channel or game does not authorize DDND to transcribe you. Voice processing is off for each account until that member reviews the `/ddnd listen` notice and explicitly confirms for their own account in the selected active voice channel. The operator cannot consent for someone else.

Use `/ddnd unlisten` to stop further processing and `/ddnd privacy` to review the notice and your status. Permission ends when you opt out, leave or move out of the channel, the session ends, or the bot disconnects or restarts. Changes to the processing providers or data-handling notice require fresh confirmation. Saved games do not restore permission.

DDND will select opted-in account streams for transcription and AI processing. Voice transport may still receive packets and connection metadata from other channel members. An opted-in microphone can capture nearby people, side conversations, or speaker audio; account selection cannot isolate individual people sharing a microphone.

Opting out discards pending audio and uncommitted transcript buffers and cancels associated work where supported. Late results will not be published or applied to the game. It cannot recall information already sent to providers or automatically delete existing saved or posted information.

Voice audio, transcripts, game context, and narration text may be sent to providers, including OpenAI, as described in the Privacy Policy. Diagnostic audio recording requires separate advance notice and agreement; game participation or transcription opt-in alone does not authorize it or public distribution of recordings.

Read the Privacy Policy for processing, storage, retention, and deletion details:

[DDND Privacy Policy](https://github.com/codextrevor-oss/ddnd-policies/blob/main/DDND-Privacy-Policy.md)

## AI responses and game outcomes

Planned text features let you submit actions, dialogue, and questions without joining voice or opting into microphone processing. Deliberately submitting text authorizes processing that submission for the game as described in the Privacy Policy, including sending relevant public or private text to configured AI providers and retaining it in game history. It does not grant voice-processing permission.

Use the designated private game-master form for secrets once available. Private submissions and replies are intended to be hidden from other players, but remain accessible to the operator and relevant service providers. They may be retained under the Privacy Policy. Direct messages are an additional route still under consideration; follow the published instructions once supported.

A private action can have observable shared-world consequences, which will be explained before commitment and disclosed only as permitted by scenario visibility rules or your explicit sharing choice. Other players may infer an action from its effects. Private requests and replies will not automatically be quoted in shared messages, recaps, or spoken narration. Posting a secret publicly and then deleting it does not make it private.

Automated systems may misunderstand speech, make mistakes, or generate inaccurate, inappropriate, or unexpected responses. Game narration is entertainment and should not be relied on as professional advice or statements of real-world fact.

Participants may request clarification, flag content, or ask to pause. The operator and the group's agreed rules govern game corrections and disputes. Dice results, fictional resources, and other outcomes have no monetary value and cannot be redeemed through DDND for money or prizes.
